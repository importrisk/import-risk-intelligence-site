Method

A judgment-driven monitoring workflow designed to surface what matters, explain why it matters, and support real decisions.

How I work
I maintain an external watchlist across policy, logistics, and geopolitical signals. I filter hard for relevance, then translate developments into operational exposure, likelihood, and time horizon.
Relevance first
No volume for volume’s sake. Signals must map to importer decisions.
Interpretation
I focus on what changes the risk picture, not what’s merely interesting.
Actionable framing
Clear “so what,” decision windows, and what to monitor next.
01

Signal intake

I track a curated set of sources across trade policy, regulatory actions, ports and carriers, sanctions, regional conflict risk, and supply constraints.

  • Policy: tariffs, exclusions, enforcement posture
  • Logistics: port conditions, carrier moves, chokepoints
  • Operational risk: strikes, weather disruption, security incidents
02

Relevance filter

Every item is scored against importer impact: who is exposed, what lanes or products, and how fast conditions could change.

  • Exposure mapping: sectors, HS codes, origin regions
  • Time horizon: immediate, 30–90 days, structural
  • Decision linkage: sourcing, inventory, contracts, routing
03

Assessment

I translate developments into a clear risk view: likelihood, severity, and leading indicators. When uncertainty is high, I say so and define what would change the call.

  • Likelihood + severity framing
  • Leading indicators to watch
  • What would confirm / negate the risk
04

Decision support

Deliverables are short, prioritized, and built for operators. The goal is to help you choose what to do and when.

  • “So what” summary and recommended posture
  • Options: mitigate, monitor, or ignore
  • Next-check cadence and triggers
Outputs
Weekly risk brief
Top changes, what they mean, and what to watch next.
Event-driven notes
When conditions shift fast: concise alerts with context and implications.
Client-specific watchlist
Sources and triggers tailored to your lanes, products, and suppliers.
What this is not

I’m not a newsfeed, a dashboard, or a firehose of links. The work is intentionally selective, interpretive, and tied to decisions.

I don’t provide legal advice, customs brokerage guidance, or compliance opinions. If you need those, I’ll suggest the right lane and the right experts.

Want this tailored to your lanes?

Tell me what you import, from where, and where you’re vulnerable. I’ll propose a watchlist and cadence.
