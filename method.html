--- layout: default title: Methodology description: How Import Risk Intelligence evaluates and tracks supply-chain risk. ---
01

Monitor

Continuous tracking of external developments that can materially affect U.S. import operations.

  • Trade policy and enforcement actions
  • Port congestion and carrier reliability
  • Geopolitical and sanctions risk
02

Assess

Signals are filtered for relevance, verified, and evaluated for real-world impact rather than theoretical risk.

  • Probability vs. severity assessment
  • Importer-specific exposure
  • Timing and escalation risk
03

Translate

Analysis is converted into decision-ready insight that aligns with planning, sourcing, and compliance workflows.

  • Operational implications
  • Cost and lead-time impact
  • Actionable scenarios
04

Support

Coverage is delivered selectively, focused on judgment and context rather than alert volume.

  • Targeted briefings
  • Ongoing risk context
  • Strategic decision support
