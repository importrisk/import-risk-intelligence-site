Methodology

How Import Risk Intelligence evaluates and tracks supply-chain risk.

Approach

I monitor external developments that can materially affect U.S. importers, focusing on relevance rather than volume.

The emphasis is on how geopolitical, regulatory, and operational events translate into real cost and execution risk.

Filtering

Signals are filtered to separate background noise from developments that warrant attention.

Coverage is selective and tailored to trade lanes, products, and regions that matter to the client.

Output

Findings are delivered as clear, concise analysis designed to support planning, prioritization, and decision-making.

This is not a 24/7 alert feed or operations desk, but focused external judgment when it matters.
