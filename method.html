Method

Senior-level judgment applied to external import risk.

Monitor

Tracking external developments affecting import operations, focused on ports, trade policy, and transportation.

Coverage is selective by design: signal over volume.

Assess

Signals are checked for credibility and relevance, separating real impact from background noise.

Likelihood and context are weighed before escalation.

Interpret

Developments are interpreted to clarify what actually changes, when it matters, and what it is likely to lead to.

The output is judgment and framing, not raw alerts.

Brief

Clients receive an asynchronous weekly executive brief, with urgent updates when immediate attention is warranted.

The goal is confidence and focus for decision makers.

Coverage In Practice

Coverage is scoped to your exposure profile and operational sensitivity, not a fixed country list or constant alerting. The focus is on developments reasonably likely to affect cost, timing, or execution.

You get decision-ready clarity for leadership without noise or unnecessary escalation, so you’re not stuck sifting through alerts, feeds, or fragmented updates.

Common Questions

Is this just a newsletter?

No. Coverage is based on what is most likely to affect your bottom line, not headline news summaries.

How is this different from alerts, feeds, or software?

Tools surface information. I apply experienced judgment to decide whether something actually matters for your business.

Does this cover supplier, sourcing, or raw-material risk?

Not directly. Those risks depend on internal system data I don’t have access to. I focus on external developments that affect import operations and cost.

What if nothing changes for a while?

You will still receive a weekly brief. Stability confirms assumptions and highlights emerging watchpoints. Preparedness matters when conditions change.

Why should I trust the analysis?

The work reflects disciplined risk assessment developed over 15 years in security and intelligence operations, combined with firsthand import experience.

How many clients do you support?

Coverage is intentionally limited, typically no more than eight clients at a time, to preserve depth and personal attention.

What do you need from us to get started?

A short intake on trade lanes, key ports, origin regions, and operational constraints. Coverage is calibrated to what would actually move cost, timing, or execution.
