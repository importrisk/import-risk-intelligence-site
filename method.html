Method

Senior level judgment applied to external risk.

Disciplined interpretation under uncertainty, without noise.

Monitor

Coverage is selective by design: signal over volume.

Assess

Signals are verified and evaluated for real-world impact, emphasizing exposure, timing, and likelihood, not theoretical risk.

Credibility and context are weighed before escalation.

Interpret

Developments are interpreted under uncertainty, including second-order effects, decision timing, and operational trade-offs.

The output is judgment and framing, not raw alerts.

Brief

Coverage is delivered selectively and calmly, emphasizing decision relevance and executive clarity rather than alert volume.

The goal is confidence and focus for decision-makers.

Common Questions

Is this just a glorified newsletter?

No. Coverage is selective and judgment-driven, focused on material risk and second-order impacts rather than summarizing headlines or producing general commentary.

How is this different from feeds, alerts, or software?

Tools surface information. Import Risk Intelligence applies judgment to determine whether a development is likely to matter for your exposure and decision-making, without dashboards to monitor or constant feeds.

Is this actually relevant to my business?

Coverage is scoped to your trade lanes, sourcing regions, and operational sensitivity. The goal is not broad geopolitical coverage, but relevance to your import exposure.

What if nothing changes for an extended period?

You will still receive a weekly brief. Periods of stability are useful signals, confirming assumptions remain intact and highlighting watchpoints that may matter next.

Why should I trust this analysis?

The work reflects disciplined risk assessment developed over 15 years in security and intelligence operations, combined with firsthand experience running an import business.

How many clients do you work with?

Coverage is intentionally limited — typically no more than eight clients at a time — to preserve consistency, depth, and senior-level attention.
