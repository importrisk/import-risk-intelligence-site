Method

Senior-level judgment applied to external import risk.

Monitor

Tracking external developments affecting import operations, focused on ports, trade policy, and transportation.

Coverage is selective by design: signal over volume.

Assess

Signals are checked for credibility and relevance, separating real impact from background noise.

Likelihood and context are weighed before escalation.

Interpret

Developments are interpreted to clarify what actually changes, when it matters, and what it is likely to lead to.

The output is judgment and framing, not raw alerts.

Brief

Clients receive an asynchronous weekly executive brief, with urgent updates when immediate attention is warranted.

The goal is confidence and focus for decision makers.

Coverage In Practice

Coverage is scoped to your exposure profile and operational sensitivity, not a fixed country list or constant alerting. The focus is on developments reasonably likely to affect cost, timing, or execution.

You get decision-ready clarity for leadership without noise or unnecessary escalation, so you’re not stuck sifting through alerts, feeds, or fragmented updates.

Common Questions

Why should I believe this is more than curated headlines?

During onboarding I confirm your operating profile and set the initial watchlist and escalation triggers. After that, each brief is what changed against that watchlist, what it means for your operations, and what I’m monitoring next.

What’s your signal threshold, and how do you avoid false alarms?

Updates are issued when conditions materially shift for cost, timing, or execution. Signals are cross-checked, framed with confidence, and escalated only when action may be warranted.

Do you track supplier, parts, or raw-material risk?

Not directly. I don't have access to your internal systems, so I don’t claim visibility into inventory, production, or supplier performance. I focus on external issues that change outcomes, then translate them into implications you can map to your planning.

How do you quantify impact instead of just describing risk?

The brief frames impact in practical terms: where delays, inspections, closures, capacity shifts, or policy changes could hit cost and timing, plus a confidence call and watchpoints that would change the outlook.

What happens if nothing changes, or the story is just noise?

You still receive a weekly brief. Stability validates assumptions, documents watchpoints, and keeps leadership aligned, so you are not reacting late when conditions shift.

We already have people watching this. Why would we pay for this?

If you already have coverage, this is additive. I focus on triaging external developments and translating them into decision impact so your team spends less time sifting and more time acting. If you don’t have a risk function, this fills that gap.

What do you need from us to get started?

A short intake on trade lanes, key ports, origin regions, product sensitivity, and decision constraints. Coverage is then tuned to what would actually move cost, timing, or execution.
