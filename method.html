Method

Senior-level judgment applied to external risk.

Monitor

Continuous observation of external developments that can materially affect import operations, with emphasis on ports, trade policy, and transportation conditions.

Coverage is selective by design: signal over volume.

Assess

Signals are verified and evaluated for real-world impact, emphasizing exposure, timing, and likelihood, not theoretical risk.

Credibility and context are weighed before escalation.

Interpret

Developments are interpreted under uncertainty, including second-order effects, decision timing, and operational trade-offs.

The output is judgment and framing, not raw alerts.

Brief

Clients receive a weekly executive brief summarizing developments that matter, with alerts issued between cycles when exposure meaningfully changes.

The goal is confidence and focus for decision makers.

Coverage In Practice

Coverage is scoped to your exposure profile and operational sensitivity, not a fixed country list or constant alerting. The focus is on developments reasonably likely to affect cost, timing, or execution, with clear framing on what to ignore, what to watch, and what may require action.

The goal

Decision-ready clarity for leadership, without noise or unnecessary escalation. Eliminating the need to sift through alerts, feeds, or fragmented updates.

Delivery

  • Weekly written briefing delivered by PDF
  • Clear framing of likely impact, timing, and confidence level
  • Updates as needed when situations materially change

What to Expect

Is this just a newsletter?

No. Coverage is selective and judgment-driven, focused on material developments and second-order effects, not headline summaries.

How is this different from alerts, feeds, or software?

Tools surface information. Import Risk Intelligence applies judgment to determine whether it is likely to matter for your exposure and decisions.

Does this cover supplier, sourcing, or raw-material risk?

Not directly. Those risks depend on internal supplier relationships and proprietary production, inventory, and system data I don’t have access to. I focus on external developments that affect import execution and cost.

What if nothing changes for a while?

You will still receive a weekly brief. Stability confirms assumptions and highlights emerging watchpoints. Nothing happening is always a good thing.

Why should I trust the analysis?

The work reflects disciplined risk assessment developed over 15 years in security and intelligence operations, combined with firsthand import experience.

How many clients do you support?

Coverage is intentionally limited, typically no more than eight clients at a time, to preserve depth and my personal attention.

What do you need from us to get started?

A short intake on trade lanes, key suppliers or regions, critical SKUs, and operational constraints. Coverage is then calibrated to what would actually move cost, timing, or execution.
