Methodology

Import Risk Intelligence focuses on external developments that create real cost, timing, and execution risk for U.S. importers.

The work prioritizes relevance and judgment over volume. Rather than continuous alerts or automated feeds, attention is directed toward events that materially affect trade lanes, sourcing decisions, and operational exposure.

Analysis integrates geopolitical developments, regulatory shifts, logistics disruption, and second-order effects to help decision-makers understand not just what is happening, but why it matters.

What This Is — and Is Not

This is not a 24/7 monitoring desk, alert service, or operations center. It is a selective risk-intelligence service designed to complement internal teams.

Coverage is focused, independent, and shaped by client exposure rather than generalized news flow.

Engagement

Coverage is tailored to specific trade lanes, sourcing regions, and product categories. Engagements are structured around the risks most likely to affect cost, continuity, and strategic planning.
