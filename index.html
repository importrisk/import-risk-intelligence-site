Approach

I provide independent risk tracking and analysis focused on external developments that can affect U.S. importers. My work centers on how geopolitical events, trade policy changes, logistics disruption, and regulatory developments translate into real operational and cost exposure.

I work independently by design. That independence allows me to focus on relevance and judgment rather than volume, automation, or constant alerts, supporting high-leverage decision-making and strategic planning as conditions change.

Scope

My work is selective and focused, intended to complement existing capabilities by providing an external perspective grounded in experience and independent assessment.

This is not a 24/7 monitoring service, alert center, or operations desk. I filter signal from noise to surface material risks, complementing internal teams rather than replacing day-to-day logistics functions.
