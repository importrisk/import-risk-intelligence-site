Not Another Dashboard

IRI is not another Saas Platform, enterprise dashboard, or stream of unfiltered noise. It is independent risk intelligence reviewed by a human and delivered for decision makers at U.S. importers.

I monitor tariff, enforcement, sanctions, and operational developments that can affect landed cost, timing, or execution, then turn that into clear analysis instead of raw updates.

Most teams already have information. The problem is knowing what matters, what changed, and what deserves attention now.

When a development is material and time-sensitive, I issue an escalation alert. Everything else is distilled into a monthly executive update covering what changed, what held, and what to watch.

How Coverage Works

Coverage is available by individual risk lane or as a full package.

Single-lane coverage includes focused monitoring in tariff, enforcement and sanctions, or operations. Full coverage combines those lanes into one external monitoring layer for importers that need a broader operating picture.

Delivery is straightforward: concise PDF alerts by email, no platform to manage, and no dashboard to monitor.

IRI is not a 24/7 alarm center or real-time crisis desk. I monitor continuously and escalate material, time-sensitive developments during defined business hours, with everything else captured in the monthly executive update.
