What Not Knowing Can Cost You

A delayed reaction can show up as added duty, emergency freight, port fees, or the cost of building this capability internally.

New tariff exposure

A 10% tariff on $8M of affected imports is an $800,000 annual hit.

One policy change can move landed cost immediately across affected goods.

Emergency freight

A delayed shipment may force high-cost expediting to protect inventory and customer commitments.

When ocean turns into air, freight cost can multiply fast.

Delay and port fee exposure

Port disruption can trigger detention, demurrage, storage, and missed delivery windows.

The financial impact often builds across several cost lines at once.
