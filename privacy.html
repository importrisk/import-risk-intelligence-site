Privacy Policy

How Import Risk Intelligence collects, uses, and protects personal information.

Effective date: February 7, 2026

Import Risk Intelligence (“IRI,” “we,” “us”) provides independent risk monitoring and analysis for U.S. importers. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1) Information we collect

  • Information you provide: Name, email, company, role, and message details when you contact us or request coverage.
  • Client communications: Operational context you share (e.g., trade lanes, ports, supplier regions, shipment timing) so we can provide analysis.
  • Basic technical data: IP address, device/browser info, and approximate location derived from IP (typical server logs).

2) How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide requested services.
  • To deliver briefings, alerts, and service communications.
  • To maintain and improve site performance, reliability, and security.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce agreements.

3) Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies or similar technologies for basic site functionality and analytics. If we use analytics, we use it to understand overall traffic and improve the site — not to build detailed profiles.

4) How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only as needed:

  • Service providers: Hosting, email delivery, and form processing tools that help us run the site and service.
  • Legal / safety reasons: To comply with law, protect rights, prevent fraud, or respond to lawful requests.
  • Business changes: If IRI is reorganized or transferred, information may be part of that transaction (with appropriate safeguards).

5) Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described above, including providing services, maintaining records, and meeting legal requirements.

6) Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to reduce risk and prevent unauthorized access.

7) Your choices

  • You can request access, correction, or deletion of your information by contacting us.
  • You can opt out of non-essential communications at any time (service messages may still be required).

8) International visitors

If you access the site from outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States where data protection laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

9) Children’s privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

10) Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above indicates the latest revision.

11) Contact

Questions or requests can be sent via the contact page.
