Pricing

Coverage can be structured by individual risk lane or as a broader monitoring package, depending on your operating exposure.

This is an external advisory service built around judgment, prioritization, and written decision support, not a software platform.

Scope options

Some companies want focused coverage around one risk area. Others need a wider operating picture across tariff exposure, sanctions, enforcement, and logistics disruption.

The options below are designed to keep scope clear.

Full Coverage

$4,000 / month

Broad monitoring across tariff, sanctions, enforcement, and operational disruption.

  • Best fit for importers that need a wider external risk picture
  • Includes cross-domain monitoring and judgment-driven escalation
  • Built for leadership visibility, not alert volume

Tariff

$1,500 / month

Focused monitoring of tariff and trade-policy developments that may affect landed cost.

  • Duty and policy changes with cost impact
  • Relevant inclusion, exclusion, and refund developments
  • Concise written escalation when something materially changes

Sanctions

$1,500 / month

Focused monitoring of sanctions and related regulatory developments that may affect sourcing, counterparties, or routing.

  • New designations and relevant enforcement shifts
  • Coverage aligned to practical import exposure
  • Clear written escalation when action may be warranted

Tariff + Sanctions

$2,500 / month

Combined coverage for companies that need visibility into both landed-cost pressure and regulatory exposure.

  • Tariff and sanctions monitoring in one package
  • Useful where sourcing and compliance risk overlap
  • More complete than a single-lane engagement without moving to full coverage
